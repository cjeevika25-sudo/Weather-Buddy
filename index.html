📖 Learn About Weather

☀️ Sunny

The Sun gives us light and heat.

☁️ Cloudy

Clouds contain tiny water droplets or ice crystals.

🌧️ Rainy

Rain happens when water droplets fall from clouds.

⚡ Thunderstorm

Thunderstorms can include rain, lightning and thunder.

💨 Windy

Wind is moving air.

❄️ Cold
